# Plynule česky

**Live site: [plynulecesky.cz](https://plynulecesky.cz)**

Website for Kateryna Leshchenko's Czech-language tutoring — individual and
group lessons, online, with the programme adapted to each student's goals.
The site is bilingual: Ukrainian (default) and Czech.

## What's on the site

- **About** — Kateryna's background: living in the Czech Republic since 2014,
  native-level Czech, several years of teaching practice with students from
  A1 to C1. Her CCE C1 state certificate and the Charles University
  methodology course certificate for teaching Czech as a foreign language are
  both linked as PDFs.
- **Specialization** — preparation for the state exams for permanent residency
  (trvalý pobyt) and Czech citizenship, preparation for university entrance
  exams, and general language competence for everyday, professional, and
  business use.
- **Testimonials** — feedback from students.
- **How to get started** — pick a time in the online calendar, book and pay on
  the site, then join the lesson on Google Meet (the link is sent
  automatically after booking). If nothing in the calendar fits, there's an
  option to get in touch by email or Telegram to arrange an individual
  schedule.
- **Pricing** — rates for 60- and 90-minute lessons, individual and group,
  with discounts for larger monthly packages. No hidden fees.
- **FAQ** — common questions about lessons, levels, and payment.
